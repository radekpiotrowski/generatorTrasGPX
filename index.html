How to make a GPX video in 3 steps

1

Upload your GPX files

Export a track from Strava, Garmin, Komoot or Veloplanner and drop it on the page. Add more tracks with "Add another GPX".

2

Set up map, colors and POIs

Choose a map style, video format and track names, then place your own points of interest on the map.

3

Click "Generate video"

GPX Alive loads the map tiles and renders a smooth video file ready for social media.

Features

🗺️

8 map styles

OpenStreetMap, a custom vector Dark Mode, Topo 3D relief shading, CyclOSM, OpenTopoMap and Satellite.

⏱️

Real-time sync

The animation reads start and end times from each GPX file, so you see the real pace and order on the route.

⛰️

Shared elevation profile

All tracks on one chart, scaled to the longest route. Turn it off for a bigger map.

📍

Custom POIs

Import waypoints from GPX, click the map or paste lat, lon coordinates. Change icons, size and labels.

📱

16:9, 9:16 and 1:1

In vertical mode the legend sits above the map so it never covers your tracks.

🔒

100% private

Your GPX files are processed in your browser's memory. Nothing is stored or sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a GPX file to a video?

Upload your GPX file to GPX Alive, choose a map style and video format, then click "Generate video". The route is animated on the map and saved as a video file.

Is GPX Alive really free?

Yes. It is free, has no ads and needs no account.

Are my GPX files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser, so your tracks stay on your device.

Can I compare several tracks in one video?

Yes. Add multiple GPX files, give each a name and color, and watch them move on the same map and elevation chart.

Where can I get GPX files?

Export them from Strava, Garmin Connect, Komoot, Veloplanner and most other sports or route-planning apps.
